Forensic Notice & Legal Presumptive Disclaimer

The classifications rendered by the NarcoTest Vision system represent objective mathematical colorimetry derived from calibrated chemical reference centroids in CIE L*a*b* color space. Chemical field test reactions are strictly presumptive and establish preliminary evidentiary basis; they do not replace definitive laboratory confirmation by Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) or Liquid Chromatography-Tandem Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS/MS). Evidentiary integrity is preserved via canonical server-side SHA-256 hashing into an append-only store.
